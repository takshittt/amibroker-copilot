---
title: Channel breakout with an ATR trailing stop
timeframe: daily
market: mcx
tags: [trend, breakout, stops, atr, long-only]
---
Buys when the close moves above the highest high of the earlier bars
and exits when the close drops below a shorter channel's low. A trailing
stop a multiple of ATR below the best price protects open profit. The
channels use earlier bars only, and trades are filled at the next day's
open.
