---
title: MACD crossover, long and short, on a market that never closes
timeframe: hourly
market: crypto
tags: [trend, macd, long-short]
---
Goes long when the MACD line crosses above its signal line and short
when it crosses below, reversing from one to the other. The market
trades around the clock, so there is no end-of-day exit. Trades are
filled at the open of the next bar.
