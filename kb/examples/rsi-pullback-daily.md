---
title: Buying an RSI pullback in an uptrend, with a stop loss
timeframe: daily
market: nse_eq
tags: [mean-reversion, rsi, stops, long-only]
---
Buys when RSI drops below an oversold level while price is still above
its long-term average, and sells when RSI recovers. A percentage stop
loss from ApplyStop limits the loss on each trade. Trades are filled at
the next day's open.
